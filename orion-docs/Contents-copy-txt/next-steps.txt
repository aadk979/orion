A node is up and a user can sign in. Here is the order most deployments take
from here.

## Harden the single node first

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add mail credentials">
    This unlocks device authorization, TOTP, passkeys, and password reset in one
    move — all four are gated on SMTP being present.
    [Configuration → mail](/versions/alpine/docs/configuration#mail)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure field encryption">
    `utilities.dataEncryption` seals TOTP secrets at rest. A single instance may
    use `provider: 'INLINE_KEY'` with a key in config; a cluster requires a key
    vault. Without either, TOTP deactivates — secrets are never written in
    plaintext, and enrolled users fall back to emailed one-time codes.
    [Key vaults & field encryption](/versions/alpine/docs/security/key-vaults)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a security tier deliberately">
    Tier 4 is the strictest and the default in the shipped template. Understand
    what each tier binds before you change it.
    [Security tiers](/versions/alpine/docs/concepts/security-tiers)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the audit trail">
    Point `utilities.auditTrailSystem` at MySQL for hash-chained, tamper-evident
    logging. [Audit trail](/versions/alpine/docs/security/audit-trail)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get behind TLS">
    Session cookies are `Secure` + `SameSite=None`; nothing works off localhost
    without HTTPS. [TLS & reverse proxies](/versions/alpine/docs/deployment/tls)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Then scale out

- [Run a cluster](/versions/alpine/docs/guides/running-a-cluster) — add Redis, list every node's
  URL in every config, and give each node a unique identity.
- [Stand up the control plane](/versions/alpine/docs/deployment/control-plane) — one orchestrator
  per cluster for health, consensus, and fleet commands.
- [Govern operator access](/versions/alpine/docs/architecture/authorization) — the PBAC
  system-admin plane puts every human behind policy with an immutable audit
  trail.

## Then go live

Work through the [hardening checklist](/versions/alpine/docs/security/hardening) end to end. It
is short, and every item on it is something that has a real failure mode behind
it.
