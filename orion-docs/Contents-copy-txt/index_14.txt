## The posture in one paragraph

Orion assumes the network is hostile and the client is untrusted. Sessions are
`HttpOnly` cookies bound to client context by a configurable security tier, and
optionally to a non-extractable browser key by DPoP. Every failed authentication
is charged to the calling actor. Every human who touches the control plane is
behind policy, two factors, and a hash-chained audit trail. Remote control of a
node is gated three times over, and a node's own safe mode always has the last
word.

      itself refuses to let you edit.

## Defaults you should know about

| Setting | Default | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `server.trustProxy` | `false` | Safe when directly exposed; silently breaks every IP-bound control behind a proxy |
| `tokens.securityTier` | `4` in the template | Strongest binding, escalates to step-up rather than rejecting |
| `tokens.binding` | `'none'` | DPoP is opt-in |
| `utilities.dataEncryption.provider` | unset | TOTP deactivates until a key vault is configured — secrets are never written in plaintext, and enrolled users fall back to emailed codes |
| `utilities.auditTrailSystem.enabled` | off unless configured | Without it, events go only to the local WAL |
| `utilities.safeMode` | `'ENABLED'` in the template | Blocks remote weakening of a node's defenses |
| `authMethods.allowedEmailDomains` | `['*']` | Anyone can register |
| `systemAdmin.enabled` | `false` | The orchestrator's power is ungoverned until you turn it on |
